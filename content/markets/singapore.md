# Singapore compensation and logistics terms

For the reasoning step and the question generator. Never read aloud, and never speak a salary band.

## Pay structure

Sales pay in Singapore is usually quoted as a monthly or annual base salary plus variable pay (commission or a
bonus at target). OTE in this screen means base plus variable at 100% of plan. Many employers pay an Annual Wage
Supplement, a "thirteenth month": it is part of fixed pay, not variable. Record what the candidate says, with the
currency; regional sellers often quote in US dollars.

## Financial year and quota windows

Most companies run a calendar financial year; some use April to March or another window. "Last year" is ambiguous
in October: record `quota_window` instead of assuming one.

## Notice

One to three months is normal, and senior sales staff often have two or three. A buyout can be negotiated, but is
not guaranteed. "Already serving notice" changes the start date.

## Work eligibility

Ask only whether the candidate can work in Singapore or would need a work pass for the role. Never ask
nationality, citizenship, residency status or where someone is from. A candidate who needs a pass is a logistics
fact for the recruiter, not a flag in the screen.

## Enterprise financial services

Banks and large financial institutions buy through long cycles (often six to twelve months or more) with
procurement, information security, outsourcing and technology-risk reviews before signature. In Singapore,
regulated institutions are expected to manage technology and outsourcing risk to the Monetary Authority of
Singapore's guidelines; a seller who has been through those reviews can name the steps.
