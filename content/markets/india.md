# India compensation and calendar terms

For the reasoning step on compensation, notice, and any rupee amount. Do not read this aloud. The performance stage uses the same units and the same financial year. Do not speak a salary band. An OTE range in the screen configuration is not the company's band.

## Units

1 lakh = 100,000. 1 crore = 100 lakh = 10,000,000. "42 LPA" means ₹42 lakh per annum. Store the amount, the currency, and the scale separately. Never add a crore figure to a dollar figure. Never convert a spoken number into dollars unless the candidate used dollars.

## CTC is not OTE

CTC, cost to company, is the employer's annual cost. It includes employer provident fund, a gratuity provision, insurance, and variable pay counted at target. It is not take-home pay. OTE in this screen means base cash plus variable at 100% of plan. It excludes employer PF and gratuity. A candidate who says "forty-two lakh" is usually quoting CTC. Do not convert CTC into OTE in your head. If the card asked for the split, record fixed and variable separately. Variable inside CTC is not guaranteed. Business Standard, 11 June 2026, defines CTC as the company's cost rather than the amount received: https://www.business-standard.com/finance/personal-finance/salary-structure-explained-basic-cost-to-company-take-home-your-salary-structure-explained-126061000860_1.html . Wisemonk's note on variable pay puts sales roles often around 20–40% of CTC, inside the CTC figure: https://www.wisemonk.io/blogs/variable-pay-in-india

Declining to name a number is allowed. It is not low effort.

## Financial year

The Indian financial year runs from 1 April to 31 March. In October 2026, "last year" can mean FY2025-26 (1 Apr 2025 – 31 Mar 2026), calendar 2025, or the trailing four quarters. Record `quota_window`. Do not assume the window from the word "annual."

## Notice

Senior notices in India are often 90 days, and 30 or 60 also occur. A buyout is sometimes possible and sometimes refused. "Already serving" changes the start date. It is not the same fact as "90 days still ahead." Talhive, 2026, on notice length and buyouts: https://www.talhive.com/blog/india-notice-period-management

## Location

The screen asks where they are based and whether the role's work arrangement and city work for them. Do not ask a US-style sponsorship question. Do not ask nationality, visa history, or caste.
