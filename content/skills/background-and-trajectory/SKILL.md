# Background and trajectory

## Objective
Establish the current role, product and buyer quickly, then how long they have been in the seat and why they are
open to a move (about 75 seconds). Context, not scored, but it changes how later answers are read: a four-month AE
cannot show a full year of attainment.

## Evidence to capture
- current_role: title AND type of company ("Senior AE at a mid-size SaaS company"). A title alone is thin.
- product: what they sell, in the candidate's words. buyer: who signs or uses it, by title.
- time_in_seat; motivation in their words. "Open to opportunities" is thin motivation, not low effort: record it.
- tenure_note (only if the CV flags short stints): the reason in their words.

## Follow-up priority
1. buyer (technical buyers matter for this role). 2. current_role (company type). 3. time_in_seat.

## Notes for the recruiter record
- Three companies in three years is a pattern; one short stint explained by a shutdown, site closure or forced
  stack rank is not. Record the reason; do not decide.

## Red flags
- Cannot name their own title or product: implausible, low effort.
- Account management described as new-logo hunting: account_management_as_new_logo.
