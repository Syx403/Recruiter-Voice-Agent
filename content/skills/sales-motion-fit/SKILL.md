# Sales-motion fit

## Objective
Quick factual fit checks against the role brief. Not scored as competence.

## Evidence to capture
- new_logo_share: share of closed deals that were new paying customers — not a new product in an account they
  already owned, not renewals. Compare with the brief's min_new_logo_share.
- segment as an employee band or typical deal-size band ("mid-market" alone does not travel); sales_cycle_days.
- territory and languages. A missing second language is not a flag unless the brief lists one.

## Follow-up priority
Ask for a number when they give an adjective ("mostly new logos" -> roughly what percent?).

## Red flags
- Mostly expansion or renewals for a new-logo role: account_management_as_new_logo.
- Every story is a nine-month enterprise cycle for a 30–120 day commercial role: a segment mismatch, even with
  fluent methodology vocabulary.
