# Compensation and logistics

## Objective
Facts for the recruiter, short and neutral: compensation, notice, location, eligibility.

## Evidence to capture
- current_comp and comp_split: current pay in the market's usual unit, and its fixed vs variable split. The market
  note says what that unit is and how it differs from OTE (for example a total-cost package that includes employer
  contributions is not OTE). Never convert one into the other.
- ote_expectation: base plus variable at plan, with currency. If they only know their current package, record it and
  the split.
- notice_period and notice_status (already resigned or not, buyout possible).
- work_location: where they are based and whether the role's location and arrangement (on-site, hybrid, remote) work.
- work_eligibility, only where the market asks it: able to work there, or would need a permit. Never nationality,
  citizenship or residency status.

## Rules
- Never speak a pay band from the screen configuration, and never present one as the company's band.
- Whether current pay may be asked is jurisdiction-specific: the market preset decides the wording.

## Engagement notes
- Politely declining compensation ("I'd rather discuss that with the recruiter") is a legitimate decline: weak but
  genuine at most, never low effort.
