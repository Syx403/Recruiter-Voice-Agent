# Sales red flags for this screen

For the reasoning step. Record a flag. Do not accuse, and do not tell the candidate the arithmetic failed. Do not invent a field. Silence on a competency is null, not a middle score.

## Attainment

Quota times the percent must be readable against deal size times deal count, in one unit. ARR, ACV, TCV, and bookings are not the same number. Multi-year TCV divided by an ARR quota is `inflated_attainment` even when the candidate can repair it on the clarify card. A repair means the true figure is now known. It does not erase the figure they led with. A team number spoken as an individual quota is a separate problem. One strong year, with the prior year unasked, is not itself a flag. The arithmetic is the same check published for hiring managers by Cornerstone Search, "Ways To Validate Sales Candidate Claims" (2026): https://cornerstonesearch.com/ways-to-validate-sales-candidate-claims/

An approximate figure or a range is acceptable. Adding amounts in different currencies or scales is not.

## Motion

A new paying account is not expansion inside an account they already owned, and it is not a renewal. When the role is a new-logo seat, a share below the role's floor is a motion flag. Do not also cut `deal_execution` for the same fact. An honest "I do not have a self-opened logo" is evidence. It is not low effort. Hunter versus farmer, as Tyler Kosik describes it at Talentfoot: the hunter can show a meeting they created; the expansion rep shows a book they were given. They are not the same seat. https://talentfoot.com/how-to-identify-elite-saas-sales-talent/

## The deal story

Only "we," after they were asked what they personally did, is evasive. A contribution percentage is not evidence. No customer number means `outcome_orientation` stays low or null. Do not award a 3 because the answer sounded commercial. A long cycle or a large ARR is a segment flag, not a character flaw.

## The objection

An absolute claim about a region, a control, or accuracy is over-promising. A clarifying question is not. Judge the answer against the objection the role's screen configuration sets, not a generic AI objection.

## Pay and person

A total-cost package spoken where OTE was asked is a unit error until they refuse to split it. Declining compensation is not low effort. Do not infer age, gender, religion, caste, or nationality from a name, a city, or a language.
