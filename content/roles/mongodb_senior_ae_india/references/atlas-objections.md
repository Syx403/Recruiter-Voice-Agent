# Atlas objections for this preset

For the reasoning step. Do not read this aloud. Do not answer the objection. The candidate is the AE. This card is residency, access, and exit for MongoDB Atlas. It is not a model-training question and not a hallucination question. Use the hallucination test only on a preset whose product is a model.

## What is publicly true

Atlas is MongoDB's managed database. The customer chooses a cloud provider and a region. MongoDB operates the service. The customer does not patch the servers. That is the point a CISO is making. Region lists change. Do not treat a recited India region as verified, and do not require the candidate to recite one. Current lists live in the Atlas cloud-provider references, for example AWS: https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/reference/amazon-aws/

Access and network, as public product facts, not as this buyer's contract: TLS in transit, encryption at rest, customer-managed keys on eligible clusters, IP access lists, VPC peering, and private endpoints. https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/security/ and https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/security-vpc-peering/ . A strong answer does not invent which of these this fintech would receive.

Exit is a logical export or a migration out, plus what the contract says. "No lock-in" is a slogan. Self-hosted MongoDB and Postgres are real alternatives on price and on control. Do not require an attack on them. The public posting also includes Professional Services for implementation. This card does not ask for a migration pitch. If the candidate volunteers one, do not treat it as the answer to residency.

## What a strong answer does

In one exchange: one clarifying question before any claim (which data, whether a region is mandatory, who signs the DPA); no invented region, certification, or "never"; a solutions architect or a security review with a near date; the commitment put in a contract schedule, not in a deck. A clarifying question is not evasion and not a missed answer.

## What to flag

"Data never leaves India." "No lock-in." "Fully compliant." A named region with no offer to confirm the current list. A speech about model training or hallucinations. Offering a deck instead of a person and a contractual next step, with no question, is a mediocre answer, not a five.
